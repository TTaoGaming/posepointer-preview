index.html, judge.js, and favicon.svg derive from letter-trace, MIT.
The stock page uses a shared guided judge and in-memory settings. The judge
retains the stock paths and thresholds and adds reach-the-dot while pressed.
sw.js replaces the letter-trace worker with a folder-scoped offline cache.

camera/ derives from the PosePointer preview engine. host.js connects the
existing camera, fingertip, filter, pen, touch priority and overlay ports
to the letter sheet. tracer-camera.js streams the pinned assets after Start.
tracer-pointer.js maps pen events to the SVG. mirror.js applies opacity in
memory. host.css places the video, hand and cursor around the paper layer.
The binding defines the sheet and an inert edge strip. The hand-skin adapter
and bundled MediaPipe runtime, model and WASM files are byte copies.
camera-button.js starts and stops the camera session and displays load progress.
